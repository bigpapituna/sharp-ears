In one minute

What happened. This week added up to 40 minutes of practice, with 51 minutes on screen in all. The piano came out on five days this week, where recent weeks averaged about two days. First finishes this week: “Drum on a lily pad” and “Copy the frogs”.

The hard bit. The app moved past one step in “Ta and ti-ti” after a few tries. Letting a step go is part of the design: the lesson is still there to come back to, and a fresh day does a lot.

Getting better. Lessons are finishing with more stars: 2.5 on average, from about 2.1 in earlier weeks.

 Say this at home: “I noticed you kept going on Copy the frogs. That's sticking power.”
