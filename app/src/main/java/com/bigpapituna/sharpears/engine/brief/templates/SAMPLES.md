# Template writer samples

What the built-in writer (`TemplateWriter`) writes on the phone, from findings the insights engine makes out of a realistic
history (`TemplateCatalogTest`, which prints these and rewrites `app/build/template-samples.md`). Mia is a pre-reader who
played twice a week and now plays six days; Roy is a grown-up learner who plays in the evening. Bullets are the brief's
facts, quoted lines the notes (each with why it is normal), "Recommended" the buttons, "To praise" the one thing to praise at
home, and the italic paragraphs the full report's sections. The child's card is what the pup says. Regenerate by running
`.\gradlew.bat :app:testDebugUnitTest --tests "*TemplateCatalogTest*"`; the titles are the real course titles.
