# Personas: Sharp Ears

These personas are hypotheses until interviews confirm them. No interview notes exist in founder/. Names, quotes and routines are invented to make the segments concrete; they are not real users.

Assumption: the location is Ottawa and the region, since the launch is Canada first and Roy lives in Ottawa.
Assumption: all three own an Android phone. This narrows the market, since the app is Android only (facts).

## 1. Marie-Eve, the parent who ends up as practice police

**Identity.** 38, Orleans (Ottawa), policy analyst. Two children, 6 and 9. Household income: [confirm in interviews].

**Day in the life.** Gets home at 5:15, cooks, and by 6:30 the children are tired. A second-hand digital piano sits in the basement. The 9-year-old had six lessons and quit; the 6-year-old asks to play "something with the dog." She hovers beside them, which is the part she hates. She uses her phone for everything else.

**Goals.** Primary: the 6-year-old plays 10 minutes a day for a month. Secondary: the 9-year-old picks it back up. Underlying: she did not get to learn and wants her children to.

**Pain points (hypothesised quotes).**
1. "If I'm not sitting there, nothing happens."
2. "I can't tell if she's playing the right notes, I never learned."
3. "I don't want an app that records my kid."

**Workarounds.** YouTube tutorials and a free app. Broken: no feedback and no reason to return. Estimate: 3 nags a week x 5 minutes = 15 minutes a week. A weekly teacher costs [local rate to confirm in interviews].

**Decision-making.** Finds tools by asking other parents and searching the Play Store. Trial is enough to try. She pays when the child asks for it unprompted. Her partner is involved above a yearly fee.

**Fit.** Urgency 4. Willingness to pay 3 (family plans cost $209 to $230 a year, see competitor-matrix.md). Reachability 3. Overall: **Primary**.

## 2. Daniel, the adult beginner who practises alone

**Identity.** 29, Vanier (Ottawa), QA analyst. Income: [confirm in interviews].

**Day in the life.** Bought a 61-key keyboard during a bad winter. Practises 20 minutes after dinner with headphones, phone on the stand. He has started Simply Piano twice and stopped both times when the songs got hard.

**Goals.** Primary: play three songs he likes by spring. Secondary: read music. Underlying: wants one thing that is not a screen for work.

**Pain points (hypothesised quotes).**
1. "I stall around the second month and don't know why."
2. "I'm not paying $170 a year to quit again."
3. "It marks me wrong and I can't tell if it's me or the mic."

**Workarounds.** Simply Piano at $169.90 a year ([ArtMaster, 13 Jul 2026](https://www.artmaster.com/articles/what-piano-apps-actually-cost-in-2026)). Broken: he drops out. Estimate: $169.90 / 52 = $3.27 a week, spent whether or not he practises.

**Decision-making.** Reddit, YouTube reviews and the Play Store. Pays after a trial, if one session shows progress. Alone in the decision.

**Fit.** Urgency 3. Willingness to pay 4. Reachability 4 (communities such as [r/pianolearning](https://gummysearch.com/r/pianolearning) and [r/AdultPianoStudents](https://gummysearch.com/r/AdultPianoStudents/)). Overall: **Secondary**. This segment is also the best served by Skoove, Simply Piano and flowkey.

## 3. Bernadette, the grandparent with a piano and visitors

**Identity.** 68, Gatineau. Retired teacher. Fixed income: [confirm in interviews]. Owns an upright she rarely plays.

**Day in the life.** Grandchildren, 5 and 8, visit most Sundays. They bang the keys, then drift to tablets. She wants something that makes the piano the visit's activity. She uses an Android phone for calls and photos, not apps.

**Goals.** Primary: the grandchildren want to go to the piano. Secondary: learn a hymn herself. Underlying: leave them with a shared memory.

**Pain points (hypothesised quotes).**
1. "They get bored in five minutes."
2. "I don't know what to teach a five-year-old."
3. "I don't want to hand them my phone and lose it to ads."

**Workarounds.** Sheet music from her own teaching days, and tablet games. Broken: no link between the screen and the real piano. Estimate: 30 minutes a visit x 4 visits a month = 2 hours of low-quality piano time.

**Decision-making.** Hears from her adult children. Pays only if her child sets it up. Her adult child is the buyer.

**Fit.** Urgency 2. Willingness to pay 2. Reachability 2. Overall: **Tertiary**. Valuable as a reason for the 8-profile plan.
