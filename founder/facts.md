# Facts the founder stated

One fact per line, with the date recorded and the skill that recorded it. Only facts Roy gave; never estimates.

- 2026-10-21 (setup): The product is Sharp Ears, an Android app that teaches piano by listening to a real acoustic or digital piano through the phone's microphone (no cable needed), with falling notes, a lesson course, games, and a coach mascot (the Golden Coach Pup).
- 2026-10-21 (setup): It was modelled on Artie (artmaster.com/artie), which the founder's notes list at $34.99 a month; Artie needs the piano connected or uses its own detection; Sharp Ears is mic-first.
- 2026-10-21 (setup): The founder is Roy, in Ottawa, Canada. He runs a short-term-rental consulting business (jstlikehome) and is learning piano himself; he builds the app with AI coding agents rather than a developer team.
- 2026-10-21 (setup): Stage: private build, not on any app store, installed on the founder's Samsung Galaxy Z Fold7 and shared with family through a private GitHub release page. No revenue, no paying users, no marketing spend.
- 2026-10-21 (setup): Current testers: the founder and his nieces and nephews (children; ages not stated).
- 2026-10-21 (setup): Shipped scope as of version 2.3.1: family profiles (up to 8 on one phone), a kids' mode with its own single-screen world map, 76 kids' game lessons across 7 pages plus a 70-lesson adult course, 39 public-domain songs, ear and rhythm games, chord tools, a sticker and reward system, a living mascot with 31 painted poses, an on-device natural voice (Kokoro, 3,500 recorded lines), wrong-key colouring, hint escalation, and crash and stuck-moment reporting.
- 2026-10-21 (setup): Everything runs on the phone; speech and audio stay on the device unless the owner sets up reports.
- 2026-10-21 (setup): The founder asked earlier whether the app could succeed in the current market and what marketing would cost; a public Play Store version is planned as a separate build (docs/publishing.md) because the private build holds the founder's imported copyrighted music.
- 2026-10-21 (setup): The founder has a debug-signed APK of about 117 MB; a shareable public download link is wanted.
- 2026-10-21 (setup): Platform: Android only (Kotlin, Jetpack Compose), minimum Android 9.
- 2026-10-21 (setup): No pricing has been decided. No fundraising is planned or stated.
