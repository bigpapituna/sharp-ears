## 2. Problem

**Headline:** Practice shouldn't need a parent on the bench

1. **"If I'm not sitting there, nothing happens."** A child starts strong, then needs you beside them for every minute. By the second week you are the one doing the work.
2. **"I can't tell if she's playing the right notes."** You never learned, so you cannot say whether the sound is right or only loud.
3. **"I don't want an app that records my kid."** Most apps ask for the microphone and say little about what they do with it.

## 3. Solution

**Headline:** The piano does the listening. The pup does the cheering.

**It listens to your real piano.** Put the phone on the music stand and play. It works with an acoustic or a digital piano, with no cable and no keyboard connection.

**A pup that makes them come back.** The Golden Coach Pup reacts to every note, naps, sneezes and cheers, in 31 painted poses. Children earn stickers and fill a world that grows with them.

**One phone, up to 8 profiles.** Every child keeps their own progress, stickers and pup. Grown-ups get their own 70-lesson course on the same phone.

## 4. How it works

1. **Install.** Get the app on an Android phone. No account.
2. **Allow the microphone.** A parent reads one plain screen and decides.
3. **Pick a profile.** Name the child and choose their age.
4. **Play the first pup game.** The child plays on the real piano and earns a sticker. The first game takes [confirm length] minutes.

## 7. FAQ

**Does the app record my child?**
No recording is kept [confirm after the public build]. The microphone is only on while a game or lesson is on screen. The sound is checked on the phone and not sent anywhere. You decide whether to allow the microphone, and you can turn it off in your phone settings at any time.

**Will it work with my piano?**
It listens to an acoustic or a digital piano through the phone's microphone. How well it hears you depends on your room and where you put the phone. We are testing in real homes now, and you can tell us what happens in yours [feedback link].

**What ages is it for?**
The pup's world is built for children from about 4 to 10 [confirm age range]. Adults and teens can use the 70-lesson course on the same phone.

**Does it need the internet or an account?**
No account. Everything runs on the phone.

**Which phones does it work on?**
Android 9 or newer. There is no iPhone version yet.
