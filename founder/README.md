## Read these first

1. **The kids' mode changes the store rules.** docs/publishing.md plans to declare "adults and teens" so Google's Families policy and COPPA do not apply. A pup world for 4 to 10 year olds is child-directed. Plan for both, and rewrite docs/privacy-policy.md, which says the app is not for children under 13.
2. **No outside family has used it.** The biggest unknowns (parents allowing the mic, children returning, a noisy room) are untested. The 14-day, 12-tester Play closed test is also your first real experiment.
3. **Price below the market, not at Artie's $34.99.** That is the highest individual monthly price found. Annual plans sit at $90 to $230 USD. See pricing-strategy.md.
